Method

How itworks

01

Data

Kaggle “Bengaluru House Data”: 13,320 listings. Rows with missing size, area or price were dropped. Ranged areas such as 1133-1384 sqft use the midpoint. Listings under 300 sqft per bedroom, with more than BHK+2 bathrooms, or with a price per sq ft far from their locality's norm were removed as data errors.

02

Model

scikit-learn GradientBoostingRegressor (250 trees, depth 4) predicting log price from locality, log area, bathrooms and BHK. Locality enters as a smoothed, cross-validated median price per sq ft, so rare areas lean on the city average. Localities with under 10 listings are not offered.

03

Runs in your browser

The trained trees are exported to a JSON file and evaluated in JavaScript. No server, no API, nothing you type leaves your device.

04

Honest limits

The listings do not include age, floor, builder, amenities or year. Two homes with the same inputs can differ a lot, which is why a range is shown. The range is the 10th to 90th percentile of errors on a held-out 20% test set. The data is a historical snapshot, not live prices. This is a statistical estimate, not a valuation.

Trained on the public Kaggle “Bengaluru House Data” set (13,320 listings). Estimates are statistical, not valuations or financial advice. Prices in ₹ lakh (1 Cr = 100 L).
